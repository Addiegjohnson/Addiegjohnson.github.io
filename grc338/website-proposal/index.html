Introduction

Spoonful of Sunshine

Spoonful of Sunshine is an all-natural organic smoothie, pressed juice, and acai bowl shop focused on fun flavors to brighten your day! It is located in Avila Beach, California, right by the beach for a perfect pick-me-up. We make everything on the menu fresh and offer make-your-own acai bowls.

Target audience

The target audience is a combination of tourists visiting the lovely area, locals, and college students. Whether it be during a sunny beach day, after a workout, or for a relaxing breakfast, Spoonful of Sunshine offers a bite to eat, fueling your day with good-quality ingredients. Students are welcome to come in for study dates with friends and relax with a yummy bite and view of the ocean.

Customers visiting the site are looking for information on our ingredients, prices, and build-your-own-bowl options. In addition, visitors will learn about what makes our shop unique compared to others and our story as a brand.

About

Spoonful of Sunshine was built on community, relaxation, and a belief that the small details matter deeply. Our goal is to bring a little pocket of joy and sunshine to your day and to curate an environment where friends can gather! Our journey began in 2010, traveling in a renovated school bus traveling around San Luis Obispo! Throughout the years, we have now settled in Avila were we proudly serve our fresh products to our loyal customers! Our doors are open for all to enjoy our delicious, fresh smoothies, juices, and acai bowls!
