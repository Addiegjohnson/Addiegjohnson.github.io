Spoonful of Sunshine was built on community, relaxation, and a belief that the small details matter deeply. Our goal is to bring a little pocket of joy and sunshine to your day and to curate an environment where friends can gather!

Our journey began in 2010, traveling in a renovated school bus traveling around San Luis Obispo! Throughout the years, we have now settled in Avila were we proudly serve our fresh products to our loyal customers! Our doors are open for all to enjoy our delicious, fresh smoothies, juices, and acai bowls!

At Spoonful of Sunshine, we believe that great taste begins with fresh, high-quality ingredients. Every smoothie, juice, and açaí bowl is carefully blended using vibrant fruits and wholesome ingredients to create flavors that are both refreshing and energizing. Our menu is inspired by the laid-back coastal lifestyle and designed to give you a delicious boost any time of day

Whether you're stopping by after a morning surf, taking a break from exploring Avila, or meeting up with friends, Spoonful of Sunshine is a place to slow down and enjoy the moment. We’re grateful for the amazing community that supports us and excited to continue sharing sunshine, smiles, and fresh flavors with everyone who walks through our doors!
